How it works

Uncertainty detection → hold → human sign-off → resume

The agent executes each step and self-scores its confidence. Any step below the approval threshold raises a hold point: execution stops, the agent states exactly what it is unsure about and what it proposes to do, and waits. You can approve as proposed, approve with guidance (your note is injected into the agent's working context for all downstream steps), or reject and force a revision. Every event — agent, human, or system — is written to a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit trail you can verify and export.
